Refund Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Thanks for choosing D's Tweaks. Please read this policy before you buy, since it explains when a refund is and isn't available.

1. Digital goods

D's Tweaks is a digital product delivered immediately after purchase. Once the software has been downloaded, sales are generally final. We don't refund for change of mind, or for incompatibility with hardware or software you didn't check before buying.

2. When we will refund

If the product doesn't work as described, we'll make it right. Contact us within 7 days of purchase if you hit any of the following:

  • The download or licence key doesn't work and we can't get it working for you.
  • The app fails to install or launch on a supported version of Windows.
  • A reproducible bug in D's Tweaks that we can't fix or work around.

We'll troubleshoot with you first. If a verified defect can't be resolved, we'll issue a refund.

3. Statutory rights

If you live somewhere with statutory consumer protections — for example the UK or the EU — those rights apply on top of this policy and are not affected by it. Paddle, as the merchant of record for your purchase, operates its own buyer terms and refund process, which take precedence where they conflict with this page.

4. How to request a refund

Open a ticket in our Discord with your order email and the Paddle order or transaction ID from your receipt, plus a short description of the problem and any screenshots. We aim to reply within 48 hours.
