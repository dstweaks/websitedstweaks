Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

D's Tweaks respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit the site or buy the software, how it is used, and who else is involved.

1. Information we collect

  • Purchase and billing data. Orders are processed by Paddle.com Market Ltd, which acts as the merchant of record for every sale. Paddle collects your email address, billing details and the location data it needs to calculate sales tax or VAT, and passes us the order confirmation and email address so we can deliver your download and provide support.
  • Technical data. Standard web logs and analytics, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, used to keep the site secure and working properly.
  • Support messages. Anything you send us through Discord or a support ticket, kept so we can help you and follow up.

2. How we use it

  • To process your order and deliver the software and any access links.
  • To send transactional email: receipts, download links and support replies.
  • To answer support requests and diagnose issues.
  • To improve the website and the product.

3. What the app itself collects

The D's Tweaks desktop application does not include telemetry and does not send your system data, usage or files to us. Optimizations are applied locally on your machine.

4. Payments and card details

We never see or store your full card details. All payment information is handled directly by Paddle under its own privacy policy and security standards. Because Paddle is the merchant of record, it is the seller on your receipt and the data controller for your payment data.

5. Third parties

We use a small number of trusted providers — currently Paddle for payments, tax and billing, and Discord for community and support. Each handles your data under its own terms. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

6. Retention

Order records are kept for as long as needed to provide support, deliver updates and meet tax and accounting obligations. Support conversations are kept only as long as they are useful.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To make a request, contact us through Discord with the email address used for your purchase.

8. Contact

For anything privacy-related, reach us in the D's Tweaks Discord.
